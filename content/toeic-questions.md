TOEIC QUESTION PATTERNS AND EXAMPLE DRILLS PARTS 1 TO 7

PART 1 - PHOTOGRAPHS

You see a picture of a man standing in a kitchen, cutting vegetables on a counter.

You hear four sentences:
A. He is eating a meal.
B. He is preparing food.
C. He is cleaning the floor.
D. He is sitting at a table.

Correct answer: B

Pattern: The correct answer is a simple and direct description of what is happening. Wrong answers use similar but incorrect actions.

What to train: action verbs in present continuous, prepositions, visual traps where a similar but wrong action is described.

Common trap types: A person described as "carrying" something when they are "holding" it. A location described incorrectly with a wrong preposition.

PART 2 - QUESTION-RESPONSE

Example 1:
You hear: "Where is the meeting room?"
A. Yes, I met him yesterday.
B. It is on the third floor.
C. The meeting was successful.
Correct: B
Pattern: "Where" questions need a location answer. Option A is a keyword trap on "met". Option C is a topic trap using the same subject.

Example 2 - the indirect reply trap:
You hear: "Did you finish the report?"
A. Yes, it was very interesting.
B. I am still working on it.
C. The report is on the desk.
Correct: B
Why: TOEIC frequently rewards natural indirect replies over literal yes or no answers. Option A sounds positive but does not answer whether it is finished. Option C states a location but does not answer the question.

What to train: Listen for the function of the question, not the keywords. "Where" needs a location. "When" needs a time. "Why" needs a reason. "Did you" questions often have indirect replies in Part 2.

PART 3 - SHORT CONVERSATIONS

You hear:
Man: "Did you send the email to the client?"
Woman: "Not yet, I am waiting for the final approval."
Man: "Alright, please send it before noon."

Question: What is the woman waiting for?
A. A reply from the client
B. Final approval
C. The email address
D. A meeting time
Correct: B

Pattern: The answer is usually stated explicitly but must be caught quickly. Read the questions and answer choices BEFORE the audio starts.

Key things to listen for: purpose, problem, next step, location, speaker role.

PART 4 - SHORT TALKS

You hear: "Attention passengers. Flight 302 to Tokyo has been delayed due to weather conditions. Boarding will begin at 3:30 PM."

Question: Why is the flight delayed?
A. Technical issues
B. Late passengers
C. Weather conditions
D. Staff shortage
Correct: C

Pattern: Listen for reason, purpose, or change. Read the questions before the audio starts to know what to listen for.

PART 5 - INCOMPLETE SENTENCES

Example 1:
The manager asked the team to submit their reports ______ Friday.
A. in   B. at   C. by   D. on
Correct: C
Pattern: "By Friday" means before the deadline. "On Friday" means on that specific day. "In" and "at" are not used with days in this context. Identify the blank type first: this is a preposition of time question.

Example 2:
She is responsible ______ managing the new project.
A. to   B. for   C. with   D. on
Correct: B
Pattern: Fixed collocation. "Responsible for" is a set phrase. This is a vocabulary and collocation question, not a grammar question.

Approach for Part 5: First identify what type of blank it is. Is it a grammar question, a vocabulary question, or a collocation question? Then eliminate wrong options quickly. Spend no more than 20 seconds per question.

PART 6 - TEXT COMPLETION

Text: "Dear Mr. Lee, We would like to thank you for your recent purchase. Your order has been processed and will be shipped ______ two business days."
A. in   B. on   C. at   D. by
Correct: A
Pattern: "In two business days" means the shipping will happen within that period. "By" would suggest a deadline. Read the surrounding sentences for context before choosing.

What to train: sentence connection, tense consistency across the paragraph, meaning within the paragraph context. Think beyond the individual sentence.

PART 7 - READING COMPREHENSION

Short email example:
"Dear Staff, The office will be closed next Monday due to a public holiday. Please complete all urgent tasks by Friday. Management"

Question 1: Why will the office be closed?
A. Staff training   B. Public holiday   C. Maintenance work   D. Company event
Correct: B

Question 2: When should urgent tasks be completed?
A. Monday   B. Friday   C. Today   D. Next week
Correct: B

Pattern: Answers are in the text but are often paraphrased. Do not read the entire passage first. Scan for key words from the question. Locate the evidence then choose.

PART 7 STRATEGY

For single passages: scan the title and first line, read the questions, go back and find the evidence.
For double and triple passages: questions near the end often require information from more than one document. Watch for "according to the email" or "based on both documents" phrases.

Time management in Part 7 is critical. If you spend more than 90 seconds on one passage, skip and return. Dying on one passage costs you marks on the ones you would have answered easily.

COMMON TRAP TYPES ACROSS ALL PARTS

Keyword trap: The wrong answer uses the same word from the question or passage but in a different context.
Similar action trap: In Part 1, a different but visually similar action is described.
Indirect reply trap: In Part 2, the correct answer does not directly say yes or no.
Topic trap: An answer that is about the same general subject but does not actually answer the question.
Paraphrase trap: In Part 7, the correct answer uses different words from the text to express the same meaning.
